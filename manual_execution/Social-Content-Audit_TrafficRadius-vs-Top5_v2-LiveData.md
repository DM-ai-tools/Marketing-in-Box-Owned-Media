**Limits, stated rather than hidden:**
- **This is each account's most recent 15 posts, not a fixed 30-day window.** For a daily poster that covers two weeks; for a monthly poster it covers more than a year. Every account has a longer history than this sample.
- **LinkedIn engagement is not published** by the company feed this reads, so it is not reported as zero or estimated. LinkedIn is compared on cadence and content only.
- **Facebook publishes no share count.** Engagement means likes + comments (+ shares where published).
- **Instagram and Facebook follower counts are not in the feed**, so engagement is shown per post, not per follower.
- **Pinned Instagram posts were left out**, because they would read as recent activity whatever their age.

None of Traffic Radius's 40 sampled posts has any of the three. All of them are anonymous, evergreen how-to graphics.

## 5. Gap Analysis — Traffic Radius

Each finding is evidenced from the sample.

1. **Volume is not the problem; it is the cost.** Traffic Radius publishes about 23 posts a week across three platforms, roughly 3–19 times its competitors (OMG, the busiest, posts about 7.6 a week; SIXGUN about 1.2). Nearly every caption is written separately for each platform (39 distinct captions, 1 shared). Engagement per post is the lowest in the set: under 1, against 10–21 on competitor Instagram. The extra volume buys no measurable attention.
2. **No video at all.** 0 reels and 0 videos across 30 Facebook and Instagram posts. Reels carry the competitors' best reach (median views 246–1,969), and the top two OMG posts were reels.
3. **No people.** The August audit's finding (no named voice) is confirmed at full sample. Not one of 40 posts names or shows a Traffic Radius person. Four of the five competitors' single best posts feature their own people; the fifth (SOUP) is an award.
4. **No proof.** The sample has no client name, result, testimonial or award. SIXGUN gets most of its engagement from exactly these.
5. **Strong assets, weak packaging.** Traffic Radius has real, ownable offers: the **Google Ads ROI Calculator** (posted 5 Oct on all three platforms) and a **funnel scorecard** (27 Sep). These are the equivalent of OMG's Gurulytics and Guru Guides. They are announced as anonymous graphics, and the calculator post earned 1 like.
6. **Seasonal content without a seasonal campaign.** The Instagram sample has a Black Friday post (30 Sep) and a Q4 post (29 Sep). OMG is already running a multi-post Black Friday series with a guide behind it.
7. **LinkedIn is used like Facebook.** 7.8 company-page posts a week to 1,783 followers. Every competitor that shows personality on LinkedIn does it through named people (SOUP, nimbl, OMG).

## 6. Recommended Content Strategy (revised)

**Principle: fewer, better, human, proven.** Cut bespoke volume and reinvest the hours in formats the competitor data shows working.

| Lever | Now | Recommended | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cadence | ~23 posts/week, each written per platform | **3–4 core posts/week**, adapted, not rewritten, for each platform | SIXGUN shares 15 of 17 posts across platforms and still averages 14 engagements on Instagram |
| Format | 100% static | **At least 1 reel per week**, plus carousels for frameworks | Reels led views for every competitor that posted them |
| Voice | Anonymous brand | **Named specialists** on camera and in captions, e.g. "Our Google Ads lead [CLIENT TO CONFIRM: name] on…" | OMG's top three posts and SIXGUN's top post name their people; SOUP's #2 names four staff |
| Proof | None | **1 proof post per fortnight**: case study, testimonial clip, award, partner badge | SIXGUN and SOUP's best posts are proof |
| Offers | Calculator and scorecard as graphics | Make them **named, recurring franchises** with a person presenting each, and a person-led CTA ("book 15 minutes with [name]") | OMG Guru Guides and Gurulytics; nimbl's named-person discovery CTA |
| Timeliness | Evergreen hygiene tips | **1 timely post per week**: a platform change, a season, a news hook | SOUP's platform-update posts; OMG's Black Friday series |
| LinkedIn | Company page, 7.8/week | Company page 2/week; **specialists post personally** 1–2 times a week each | Mirrors the Partner Voice mechanic already scoped in the LinkedIn sub-service brief |

**Suggested weekly rhythm** (4 core posts):
1. **Reel, a named specialist:** one sharp point in 30–45 seconds, e.g. "$40 lead at 5% vs $80 lead at 20%", adapted from the existing caption that has the strongest hook.
2. **Carousel framework:** the scorecard or a checklist, ending on the calculator or scorecard CTA.
3. **Proof or culture post:** client result, testimonial, award or team moment.
4. **Timely post:** this week's platform or seasonal change, Traffic Radius's take, and what to do.

**Measure it the way this audit measured it.** Re-run the same SociaVault fetch in 30 days, at about 48 credits. The test passes if mean Instagram engagement per post moves from under 1 to 5 or more, and any reel exceeds 250 views (the competitor floor).
