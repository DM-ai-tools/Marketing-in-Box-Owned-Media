VOICE PACK — E-COMMERCE / RETAIL (online stores, DTC brands, consumer products)

Tone register
- Energetic, sensory and customer-first. Short, scannable copy that sells the product experience and removes purchase friction.
- The brand's own personality leads; benefits over features, but specifics (materials, sizes, shipping times) close the sale.

Compliance posture
- Consumer-law basics: prices, discounts and "was/now" claims must be genuine and stated from INPUTS, never invented. Shipping, returns and warranty terms are stated from INPUTS or left as marked placeholders.
- Product claims (health, eco, "made in", certifications) only if INPUTS confirm them.
- Urgency and scarcity are acceptable only when real (a genuine sale end date or stock level supplied in INPUTS).
- Reviews and ratings are never fabricated; use placeholders where the client must insert real ones.

Vocabulary
- Prefer: "shop", "customers", "free shipping over…" (if true), "easy returns", "in stock", "add to cart", "bestseller" (if substantiated), product-specific sensory words.
- Avoid: B2B consulting language ("engagement", "strategy session", "discovery call"), agency jargon, invented social proof.

Claim aggressiveness
- Moderate-to-bold on product experience, strict on factual claims (price, stock, reviews, certifications).
