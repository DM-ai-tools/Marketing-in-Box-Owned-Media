VOICE PACK — REGULATED FINANCE (lending, broking, advice, insurance, wealth, accounting-adjacent credit)

Tone register
- A careful, plain-spoken advisor, not a growth marketer. Calm, specific, measured. Confidence comes from clarity and process, never from hype.
- Short declarative sentences. Explain terms the first time they appear. No exclamation marks.

Compliance posture
- Treat every claim as a financial promotion that a regulator may read. Never promise approval, returns, savings, rates or outcomes; say what the client does and what depends on the reader's circumstances.
- Any rate, fee, saving or comparison needs its basis stated or must be left as a placeholder for the client to substantiate — never invent a number.
- Include the need for a general-advice / "consider your own circumstances" style disclaimer and eligibility / credit-criteria language wherever a product or outcome is described. Leave the exact licence number and disclaimer wording as a marked placeholder for the client's compliance team.
- No urgency or scarcity pressure ("only today", "don't miss out", countdowns) around a credit or investment decision.
- Testimonials and case outcomes: only if INPUTS permit them, and never framed as typical results.

Vocabulary
- Prefer: "may", "subject to eligibility", "assess", "options", "structure", "fees and charges", "lending criteria", "your situation", "speak with a specialist".
- Avoid: "guaranteed", "instant approval", "risk-free", "best rates", "free money", "skyrocket", "crush", "10x", "hack", "growth engine", agency jargon (funnels, leads, CTR) in reader-facing copy.

Claim aggressiveness
- Conservative by default. Proof is process, credentials, years in operation, panel/lender access and transparent fees — not outcome superlatives.
