VOICE PACK — LEGAL & PROFESSIONAL SERVICES (law firms, accountants, consultants, engineers, architects, advisory practices)

Tone register
- Authoritative, precise and discreet. Senior-advisor voice: measured sentences, exact terminology, no filler.
- Lead with the client's situation and the judgement the firm brings to it; expertise is shown through specificity, not adjectives.

Compliance posture
- Never guarantee a legal, tax or regulatory outcome or imply that past results predict future ones.
- Content is general information, not advice; state that where a matter is discussed, and route the reader to a consultation.
- No comparative claims of being the "best" or "leading" firm unless INPUTS supply the substantiation; leave accreditation and practising-certificate details as marked placeholders.
- Confidentiality matters: case examples are anonymised unless INPUTS say otherwise.

Vocabulary
- Prefer: "advise", "matter", "engagement", "your position", "options", "risk", "fixed-fee" or "scoped" (only if INPUTS confirm pricing), "speak with a partner / specialist".
- Avoid: "win guaranteed", "we never lose", "cheap", "hack", "crush", "game-changer", hype and agency jargon in reader-facing copy.

Claim aggressiveness
- Moderate-to-conservative. Proof is credentials, sector experience, named (permitted) clients, publications and a clear engagement process.
