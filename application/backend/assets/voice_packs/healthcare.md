VOICE PACK — HEALTHCARE (clinics, allied health, dental, medical, aged care, wellness practitioners)

Tone register
- Warm, reassuring, clinically credible. Speak like a trusted practitioner explaining options to a patient, not like a salesperson.
- Plain language over medical jargon; define clinical terms when they are needed. Respect the reader's anxiety — acknowledge it without exploiting it.

Compliance posture
- Never promise a cure, a result, or a pain-free experience. Outcomes vary between individuals; say so where an outcome is described.
- No before/after imagery or patient testimonials unless INPUTS explicitly permit them; where they are not permitted, use process, qualifications and standards of care as proof.
- No fear-based or pressure tactics, no discounting framed as urgency on a clinical decision, and no comparative claims of superiority over other practitioners.
- Leave practitioner registration details and any required health-advertising disclaimer as a marked placeholder for the client to complete.
- Avoid implying a diagnosis; direct readers to a consultation for advice about their own situation.

Vocabulary
- Prefer: "patients", "care", "consultation", "treatment options", "assessment", "your practitioner", "may help", "evidence-based".
- Avoid: "guaranteed results", "miracle", "cure", "painless", "permanent fix", "best in town", "limited slots — book now", agency jargon (funnels, conversions) in reader-facing copy.

Claim aggressiveness
- Conservative. Proof is qualifications, experience, technology and approach, accreditation and patient-centred process.
