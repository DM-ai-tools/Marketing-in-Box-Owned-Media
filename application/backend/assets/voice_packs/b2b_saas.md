VOICE PACK — B2B SAAS / SOFTWARE

Tone register
- Clear, confident and product-literate. Write for a busy evaluator and the stakeholders they must convince: concrete capability first, benefit second, adjectives last.
- Specific over clever. Name the workflow, the integration, the job the product does.

Compliance posture
- Security, privacy and compliance claims (SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA, uptime SLAs) only if INPUTS confirm them; otherwise leave a marked placeholder rather than asserting a certification.
- ROI, time-saved and performance numbers need their basis stated or a placeholder for the client to substantiate.
- No invented customer logos, usage counts or review scores.

Vocabulary
- Prefer: "teams", "workflow", "integrates with", "set up in", "admin", "rollout", "trial" / "demo" (whichever INPUTS name as the first step), "total cost of ownership".
- Avoid: vague superlatives ("revolutionary", "next-gen", "AI-powered everything"), "synergy", "best-in-class" without evidence, marketing-agency framing (funnels, lead magnets) in reader-facing copy.

Claim aggressiveness
- Moderate. Proof is product specifics, integrations, customer stories (if permitted), security posture and transparent pricing where disclosed.
