VOICE PACK — LOCAL SERVICE BUSINESS (trades, home services, cleaning, automotive, fitness studios, salons)

Tone register
- Friendly, direct and trustworthy — the voice of the owner who turns up on time. Plain words, short sentences, no corporate language.
- Local and specific: name the suburbs / service area from INPUTS, the jobs done, how fast they respond.

Compliance posture
- Licences, insurance and qualifications are stated only from INPUTS; otherwise leave a marked placeholder (for example "[Licence no.]").
- Prices and quotes: follow the INPUTS pricing-disclosure mode; never invent a price or a "from $" figure.
- Guarantees ("satisfaction guaranteed", "workmanship warranty") only if INPUTS confirm the client offers them.
- Reviews are never fabricated; reference the client's real review platform or leave a placeholder.

Vocabulary
- Prefer: "call us", "free quote" (if offered), "same-day" (if true), "local", "licensed and insured" (if true), "your home / your car", suburb and region names.
- Avoid: agency and B2B jargon ("solutions", "leverage", "funnel", "synergy", "scalable"), vague superlatives without proof.

Claim aggressiveness
- Moderate. Proof is reviews count/rating (real), years operating, licences, response times and before/after work photos if INPUTS permit.
