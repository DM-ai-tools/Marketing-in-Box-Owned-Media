SUB-SERVICE SCOPE (Phase 2 — read before anything else below)

This ICP is for ONE SUB-SERVICE, named in the "Sub-Service + Price/Terms" input — not for the client's whole business and not for the broader headline service it sits under (for example "Meta Ads", not "Social Media Marketing" or "Digital Marketing"). It is built from scratch for that sub-service. No earlier ICP is an input to it, and none should be assumed.

1. The avatar is the buyer of THIS sub-service. Profile the person who would search for, compare and pay for it specifically — which is often a different person, with a different trigger, budget line and level of sophistication, from the buyer of the client's other services.

2. Every service-level section is written about the sub-service: the pains it solves, the outcomes it promises, the objections raised against it, the alternatives it is compared with (including doing it in-house or with a specialist provider of only this sub-service), the proof that persuades, and the decision criteria. Do not drift up to the headline service or across to sibling sub-services.

3. Client-level facts still apply unchanged: company name, company type, region, business model, audience orientation and any compliance constraints describe the client, not the service.

4. Name the sub-service explicitly in the avatar's own words where it is natural, and use the jargon of the sub-service's own channel or discipline rather than generic marketing language.

5. Before output, check: a reader who knows only this ICP could tell which sub-service it is for.

    B2C: trust signals, proof, convenience, outcomes, financing, aftercare.
